---
title: Choosing an auth method
description: Match the method to the device
sidebar_position: 1
---

Real deployments mix devices. **Match the method to what each device can do.**

| Method | Suited to |
| --- | --- |
| EAP-TLS | Devices that hold a certificate. Strongest |
| EAP-SIM / AKA | SIM-equipped (cellular) |
| PEAP / TTLS | Username and password |
| MAB | MAC only. **Identification, not authentication** |

:::warning[MAB is a last resort]
A MAC address is claimed, never proven. Restrict its reach with VLANs or ACLs.
:::
