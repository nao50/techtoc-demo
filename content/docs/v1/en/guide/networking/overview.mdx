Split network design into two questions:

1. **Where do devices connect** (access points, PGW)
2. **Once authenticated, where can they go** (VLAN, ACL)

## Where they can go

After authentication, assign the device to a network. See
[VLAN assignment](/demo/en/docs/guide/networking/vlan).

:::note
"Can it connect" and "where can it go" are separate decisions. Even if a
device connects, keeping its reach narrow limits the damage if it is compromised.
:::
